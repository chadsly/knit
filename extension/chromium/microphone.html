Enable your microphone for Knit

Choose Enable microphone, then allow Chrome's permission prompt. This checks access only: Knit will not record or save audio here.

If access is blocked, check Chrome Settings > Privacy and security > Site settings > Microphone. On macOS, also check System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone and enable Google Chrome.

After allowing access, return to the Knit side panel and press the microphone button to start recording.
